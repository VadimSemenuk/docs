Account and Data Deletion

Application: I am fine

Developer: mamindeveloper

How to request account deletion

Method 1. Through the app

  1. Open the I am fine app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Select Delete account
  4. Confirm deletion

Method 2. By email

  1. Send an email to: mamindeveloper@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject: Account deletion
  3. Include the email address used to register in the app

What data is deleted

When an account is deleted from the I am fine app, all associated user data is permanently removed, including:

  • email address
  • username
  • user settings
  • app usage data

Data retention and timing

  • Account and data deletion is performed immediately after confirmation
  • Data is not stored or archived
  • Data retention period after deletion: 0 days

The account exists only within the I am fine app and is not used in any other services by the developer.
